# Privacy Policy for Pocket Finance Ledger

**Last updated: September 24, 2026**

Pocket Finance Ledger is an iOS and iPadOS personal finance application provided by Mykola Piven. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information.

## Summary

Pocket Finance Ledger does not require an account and does not send personal or financial information to the developer or to an application server. Ledger entries, account information, categories, settings, and receipt images are stored locally on the user's device.

The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party software development kits that collect user data.

## Information entered into the app

Users may choose to enter or create information such as:

- income and expense records;
- merchant names, amounts, dates, categories, and transaction status;
- account names and balances;
- receipt images selected from the photo library or captured with the camera;
- app preferences, including currency display, theme, and privacy mode.

This information is used only to provide the app's ledger, receipt scanning, search, balance, and analytics features. It remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

## Camera, photos, and receipt recognition

Camera access is requested only when the user chooses to capture a receipt. Photo access is used only when the user selects an image through Apple's system photo picker.

Receipt recognition is performed on the device using Apple system frameworks. Selected or captured receipt images may be saved in the app's local storage when the user attaches them to a ledger entry.

## Data collection and sharing

Pocket Finance Ledger does not collect, sell, rent, disclose, or share personal data, financial data, receipt images, device identifiers, or usage information with the developer, advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers.

The app does not use data for cross-app or cross-website tracking.

## Local storage and backups

App data is stored locally using Apple platform storage and file-protection features. Depending on the user's device and backup settings, app data may be included in an iCloud or computer backup managed by Apple and the user. Those backups are governed by Apple's terms and privacy practices.

No security method can guarantee absolute protection, but the app uses the storage protections provided by iOS and iPadOS.

## Data retention and deletion

Information remains on the device until the user edits or deletes individual records, uses the app's ledger reset feature, or removes the app. A user can also manage or delete device backups through Apple account or device settings.

Because the developer does not receive or maintain the user's ledger data, there is no server-side account or database from which the developer can retrieve or delete it.

## External links and support

The app may open this Privacy Policy in the system browser. The public support repository and issue tracker are hosted by GitHub. Information voluntarily submitted to GitHub is handled according to GitHub's own terms and privacy policy. Users should not include financial information, receipt images, balances, or other sensitive data in support requests.

## Children's privacy

Pocket Finance Ledger does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users. All financial information entered into the app remains stored locally as described above.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's features or privacy practices change. The latest version will remain available at this URL, with the revision date shown at the top.
